What Are the Drawbacks to Staying Connected?

Every post, message and even content watched can create data. How much data do these major social media apps say they collect?

Explore data
01

Background

What qualifies as personal data?

Personal data can include obvious things like your email addresses, current location, photos saved to your phone, your contacts and even less visible information like device identifiers, search history, and app usage.

Why collect do they it?

Platforms use data to operate certain features, recommend personalized content, show relevant advertising, measure engagement, and improve their products. That same data can also create privacy risks when the companies collect large amounts and do not fully disclose the reasons why.

Being connected doesn't only describe people; it can also refer to the connections between accounts, devices, advertisers, and behavioral profiles.

Findings & Reflection

1

Collection is broad

Most apps show multiple categories, not just the information people are aware of.

2

Labels require context

When a label states that data may be handled it doesn't reveal frequency, retention, or every use.

3

Privacy is a trade-off

Convenience and personalization can be cool and helpful, but people should carefully decide whether each permission allowed is necessary.

My reflection

The most interesting thing I learned was that there are so many more categories tracked than the average person is aware of. Just opening the app can give the company so much data like location, search history and more. I also learned that the privacy labels are often times not showing the whole picture because they are always created by the companies and are always subject to change.

In conclusion, the major drawback to staying connected is that every time you are the app will take more and more of your data to create an accurate image of you based on your likes dislikes, etc. To combat this acquisition of unnecessary data, you can turn off many unnecessary permissions. For example, location is not always required so turning it off can limit data collection for a number of apps.
