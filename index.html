FINAL RESEARCH PROJECT

What Are the Drawbacks to Staying Connected?

Every post, message and even content watched can create data. How much data do these major social media apps say they collect?

Explore data
01

Background

What qualifies as personal data?

Personal data can include obvious thigns like your email addresses, current location, photos saved to your phone, your contacts and even less visible information like device identifiers, search history, and app usage.

Why collect do they it?

Platforms use data to operate certain features, recommend personalized content, show relevant advertising, measure engagement, and improve their products. That same data can also create privacy risks when the companies collect large amounts and do not fully disclose the reasons why.

“Connected” does not only describe people. It also describes the links among accounts, devices, advertisers, data brokers, and behavioral profiles.

Findings & Reflection

1

Collection is broad

Most platforms disclose multiple categories, not just the information users actively post.

2

Labels need context

A label says data may be handled; it does not reveal frequency, retention, or every downstream use.

3

Privacy is a trade-off

Convenience and personalization can be useful, but users should decide whether each permission is necessary.

My reflection

The most surprising finding was how many categories extend beyond what a person intentionally posts. Identifiers, diagnostics, usage patterns, and location can reveal behavior even when a user shares very little publicly. I also learned that privacy labels are useful but incomplete because they are developer-reported and can change by region, age, and feature.

My conclusion is that the drawback to staying connected is not one single piece of data. It is the combination of many small signals into a detailed profile. Users can reduce exposure by reviewing permissions, turning off unnecessary location access, avoiding contact syncing when it is not needed, limiting ad tracking, and periodically deleting unused accounts.

Sources & Method

Method: I recorded broad data categories disclosed in the U.S. Apple App Store privacy labels for ten apps, then compared the number and sensitivity of categories. App-store disclosures may change and are not independently verified by Apple.
